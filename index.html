Overview

A villa built for a full house, not a couple's getaway

Casa Europa is a newly renovated beach villa a two‑minute walk from Calangute beach and five minutes from Baga. The rooftop swimming pool — 7 by 3 metres, under three feet deep — is exclusively for guests, ringed by sunbeds and an alfresco dining area that catches the evening light.

Downstairs, a self‑catering European kitchen and indoor and outdoor lounges give the ground floor room to spread out; upstairs, all six bedrooms are air‑conditioned and en‑suite, each with a smart‑screen TV and its own balcony. Self check‑in is by keypad, so arrival doesn't depend on anyone's schedule but yours.

It's a house that works equally well for a multi‑family holiday, a wedding party, or a group who want their own kitchen, their own pool, and Calangute on foot.
